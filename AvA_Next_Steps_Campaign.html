Six steps, in order — and why each one exists

Step 1 · The Proof Story — 78% to 89% in 90 Days

Week of Aug 24
A: "How one community found 11 points"B: "The 90-day census climb, step by step"

The sequence opens with the occupancy narrative that has tested best in this program — a community's climb from 78% to 89% in 90 days, told by the numbers. A/B subject test decided by open rate. And as it sends, Steven is already on the phone: he personally follows up with the technology chiefs, HR leaders, and Quality and Clinical Integration leaders who clicked during the Main series — most active clickers first, by phone and direct message — so the program's warmest hands hear a human voice the same week the new sequence opens.

The logicThe 78→89 framing earned the lead in earlier testing — and in a program where openers convert at half-or-better, leading with the strongest proof puts the best foot forward and gives every later send a data-informed head start.

Step 2 · The Personal Video Introduction

Week of Aug 31
A: "90 seconds on the move-ins that never called back"B: "I recorded this about your follow-up gap"

Steven on camera, under 90 seconds, on census and the follow-up gap that quietly costs communities move-ins. Thumbnail with play button; one clear ask.

The logicVideo lands hardest on an audience that already trusts the sender — which is exactly what the proof story creates. Arriving second, the personal introduction meets a warmed room, where personalized video does its best work: benchmarks put it at roughly +40% opens and multiples on response.

Step 3 · The Direct Ask

Week of Sep 7

Short and direct — one clear ask for a conversation, with the video and walkthrough one click away for anyone arriving fresh.

The logicTwo rounds of live data shaped this slot. An audience that clicks through at half-or-better once it opens has earned a direct request — hedging here would waste the very conversion strength the program proved.

Step 4 · The Clicker Follow-Up — Automated

Always on · within 48h of any click

Any click, on any send, triggers a tailored follow-up within 48 hours, referencing what they clicked — no human in the loop. Built as a workflow and seed-tested before launch.

The logicThe behavior-triggered follow-ups were the Main series' best performers at 50–67% click-to-open. A result that consistent gets promoted from practice to infrastructure: automated, immediate, and incapable of being forgotten on a busy day. Every click is a signal of intent, and this step answers every one of them.

Step 5 · The Reader Follow-Up

Week of Sep 14

A tailored touch for readers who opened any send but haven't yet clicked — a different door into the same story. Segment defined as "opened any send in this campaign, clicked none," count verified the day before it fires.

The logicIn a program where half of openers click, the opener who hasn't clicked yet is the highest-value remainder in the file — clearly interested, one nudge from acting. They get a fresh angle rather than a rerun, and the segment count is checked before firing because precision is the whole point of the play.

Step 6 · The Finale

Week of Sep 14–18
A: "Last note from me (for now)"B: "One more thing before I go quiet"

A clearly-final send — honest, unhurried, unmistakably the last one.

The logicA real ending concentrates decisions — the clearly-final pattern has consistently earned a late wave of engagement wherever it has run. It also respects the reader, which is the posture this whole program is built on.
Three lanes running alongside the emails — all in service of growing the room

Lane 1 · Sales Navigator — prospecting the proven committee

Saved searches run weekly against the exact titles the Main series proved will engage. New prospects enter a connect-then-converse cadence; a prospect graduates to the email list only with a working email and a legitimate basis to contact. This lane is the campaign's growth engine, and it compounds week over week.

Search 1 — Technology (primary) "Chief Information Officer" OR "CIO" OR "Chief Technology Officer" OR "VP Information Technology" OR "VP of IT"
Search 2 — People & Culture "VP Human Resources" OR "VP of HR" OR "VP Organizational Development"
Search 3 — Quality & Clinical "Director of Quality" OR "Clinical Integration" OR "VP Social Services" OR "Life Enrichment"

Cadence: Day 0 — personalized invite, no pitch · Day 3 — thank-you with one useful observation, no link · Day 7 — the 90-second video, shared as a courtesy · Day 12 — the conversation ask. Weekly volume stays comfortably inside platform guardrails.
The logicMain 4 proved technology leadership belongs at the front of this list — they arrived in force the moment the message reached them, so Search 1 is the primary search, not an afterthought. Four distinct HR VPs clicking earned Search 2 its own lane. The no-pitch cadence reflects how senior committee members actually connect: with people first, material second.

Lane 2 · The existing network — the warm layer

Steven's LinkedIn relationships matched against the committee profile — technology, HR, quality, and operations leadership in senior living — imported to the email audience as a named, verified list, with personal notes to the closest matches before the campaign opens.

The logicIn a program whose one constraint is room size, the fastest room-growth available is the network that already exists. A verified, name-by-name import also protects deliverability — every contact is real, current, and legitimately reachable.

Lane 3 · Steven's personal outreach — the closing lane

It opens before the first email does: Steven personally circles back to the last campaign's most-active clickers — the highest click-volume contacts, most clicks first — by phone and direct message. They are the warmest hands in the file, and they hear a human voice before anything new arrives in their inbox.

Then, as the new sequence runs: Every clicker lands on the next morning's call sheet, ranked by total clicks — the multi-click HR and Quality leaders from the Main series open the queue. Steven calls within 48 hours of a click, five calls per day, each with a one-line opener tied to what the person clicked. Outcomes are logged in a line; voicemails retry in three business days, three attempts max. New connection acceptances get a personal message within a day.

The logicWhen half your openers click, the click list is the pipeline — it deserves a human voice, fast, while intent is warm. The 48-hour window matches the automation's rhythm so the email touch and the personal touch arrive as one motion, and the five-call cap keeps the practice sustainable every single day.
