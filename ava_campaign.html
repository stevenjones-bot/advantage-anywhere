How it runs

Reply is the only ask.No calendar links, no forms. Every email ends with Steven's address and one line: reply.
Clicks get a person, fast.Anyone who clicks a film gets the "Thanks for clicking" cut within 48 hours, once — then Steven follows up by hand.
Nobody hears the same thing twice.People who click drop out of the later sends; people who open but never click get their own note.
Five-day rule.Every send is judged on day five, never sooner; the next one is shaped by what the last one taught.
